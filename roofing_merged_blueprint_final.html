Why this order is not random

Sections 1–2 must work above the fold to stop the bounce. Sections 3–7 build a case progressively — evidence, then process, then social proof, then services — so the visitor arrives at the mid-page breather already 80% convinced. Sections 9–10 handle the final objections. Section 11 closes the fully-convinced visitor. The order follows the psychology of trust, not personal preference.
